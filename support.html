Support

Help with Meal Snap, your account, and your subscription.

Contact us

Email weletpojulu@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Meal Snap Pro. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account and data

In the app, go to Profile > Delete account. Your personal data, including photos, is deleted within 30 days.

Disconnect Apple Health

Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, and choose Meal Snap to change or remove access.
